About me

More than a designer. A builder.

I'm a graphic designer and web developer originally from Honduras, with a background in visual identity, web design, and brand systems. I graduated with an A.S. in Graphic Design from Broward College.

I don't take every project. I take the ones where design can actually change something — for a brand, a business, or a person. My work spans from brand identities to fully coded websites, always built with intention.

Bilingual in English and Spanish. Based in San Pedro Sula, Honduras.
